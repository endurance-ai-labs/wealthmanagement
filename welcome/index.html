The problem

A practice this size runs on whatever the broker-dealer gives you.

Performance reporting from one vendor, planning from another, the cross-border tracking in a spreadsheet only Kent maintains. It works until someone asks a question at 4pm on a Friday, or until a household moves abroad and nobody is sure what that triggers.

01

Every number derives

A household's value is the sum of its accounts, which is the sum of its positions, which is the sum of its tax lots. Revenue is the fee schedule applied to assets. Nothing is typed twice, so nothing can disagree.

02

The cross-border work is tracked, not remembered

Which households have obligations abroad, what currency they are exposed to, what gets triggered and when. Every fund on the platform is US-domiciled, so nothing becomes a PFIC by accident.

03

Approvals are controls

A billing run, a policy change, a report release, a plan sponsor's fiduciary review: each moves through a chain that will not advance without the right signature.
